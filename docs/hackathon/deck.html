01The problem

Retail trading bots fail people in three ways

They overtrade

Intraday signals are mostly noise, and fees compound against you on every trade.

We built 7 intraday strategies first — none had an edge after 0.1% fees.

Their track records can't be verified

Screenshots and backtests are easy to cherry-pick. Nobody can prove when a decision was made.

Trust today = “take our word for it”.

They want your keys

Custodial bots and exchange API keys put your funds at someone else's risk.

Your money should stay in your wallet.
02The solution

One slow, rule-based decision a day — executed on Solana, proven on-chain

After each daily close, compare the price with its 20, 50, 100 and 200-day averages. The share of averages below the price is the share held in the coin.

03How it works

From a daily candle to a confirmed swap

Daily close

Public daily candles for BTC, ETH and SOL.

Signal

SMA ensemble

4 averages vote → target share per coin.

Plan

Rebalance plan

On-chain

Memo proof

Signals + plan published to Solana first.

Jupiter

Swap & confirm

Quote → signed tx → confirmed → balances booked.

Fills are booked from on-chain balance changes, not from quotes
No double buys: a lost send is resolved by signature until blockhash expiry
Idempotent daily decision: restarts never trade twice

          08Built to be trusted
          
Safety and engineering

          

          

            

              
              
Non-custodial

              
Funds never leave the user's wallet. Real swaps need an explicit BOT_ALLOW_SOLANA_REAL flag.

            

            

              
              
Risk-free simulation

              
Live Jupiter quotes, routes and price impact — without sending a single transaction.

            

            

              
              
Exact accounting

              
Fills come from confirmed transaction balance deltas, not from the quote.

            

            

              
              
Auto-protection

              
Sells everything and stops if capital falls below a chosen threshold.

            

            

              
              
170+ automated tests

              
Swaps on a simulated RPC, blockhash expiry, lost sends, memo size limits, 3 languages.

            

            

              
              
Diagnostics built in

              
Structured journal with operation ids, secrets masked, one-command incident report.
